Glenmore Central is a 5-storey, 105-unit condo building at 722 Valley Road, completed in fall 2020 by Westpoint Projects — one of the newest condo buildings in Glenmore.

The unit mix runs from studios through 2-bedroom layouts, giving it a wider range of buyer types than most of Glenmore's older, family-scaled buildings.

Being only a few years old, Glenmore Central trades on modern finishes and current building code standards rather than the character-home appeal of older Glenmore stock — a genuinely different pitch depending on what you're after.
