55+ Age-Restricted Building
Glenmore Place is a 55-and-over strata community. Buyers must meet the age requirement to purchase here — worth confirming before you fall in love with a unit.

Glenmore Place is a 30-unit, 55-and-over condo complex at 1490B Gordon Drive, built in 1982. Units range from roughly 1,069 to 1,670 square feet, mostly 2- and 3-bedroom layouts — larger than typical for a building of this era, and part of why it draws downsizers who still want real living space.

The Gordon Drive location puts residents near Glenmore's established amenities, roughly a five-minute drive from downtown Kelowna and about ten minutes from the airport — a convenient, central position for buyers who don't want to be far from either.

As an age-restricted building built over four decades ago, Glenmore Place trades in a distinct market from Glenmore's newer, unrestricted condo stock — pricing here reflects unit size and location more than modern finishes. We can walk you through exactly how that trade-off plays out in current asking prices.
