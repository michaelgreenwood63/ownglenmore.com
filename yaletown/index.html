Yaletown is a 122-unit condo complex spanning several wood-frame buildings along Glenmore Road North, addressed 1425 through 1483, built in 2008 by Troika Developments. Individual buildings within the complex are typically referred to by letter — Yaletown A, B, C, D, and so on — each 3 storeys.

Because Yaletown is really a multi-building complex rather than a single tower, unit values can vary by which specific building a listing is in, in addition to the usual floor and view factors. Knowing which building you're looking at matters here more than it does in a single-tower development.

As a 2008-built complex, Yaletown has plenty of resale history at this point — useful for pricing confidently across its various buildings rather than treating the whole address range as a single flat market.
