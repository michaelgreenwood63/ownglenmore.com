SandPointe is a gated, 55-and-over community on Yates Road in Glenmore, built around single-level ranchers and condo-style units. Gated access and an age restriction make it a genuinely different market from Glenmore's family-oriented condo stock nearby.

The clubhouse is the centrepiece — both indoor and outdoor pools, giving residents a resort-style amenity most Glenmore buildings this size don't have.

As with any age-restricted, gated strata, rules and fees run differently than an open-market condo — we can walk you through exactly what applies before you commit to a unit here.
