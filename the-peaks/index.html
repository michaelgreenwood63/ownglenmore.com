The Peaks is a 35-unit townhome complex at 1455 Cara Glen Court, one of the more recently completed additions to Glenmore's townhome stock.

Units run 2- and 3-bedroom-plus-den layouts, most with rooftop patios and double garages — a genuinely spacious, family-oriented format compared to a lot of newer, denser townhome builds elsewhere in Kelowna.

As a recent build, exact completion timing and remaining strata warranty coverage are worth confirming unit-by-unit — we can pull that detail before you make an offer.
