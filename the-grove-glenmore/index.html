The Grove is a 3-storey, wood-frame townhome complex at 300 Drysdale Boulevard, at the corner of Drysdale Boulevard and Glenpark Drive — 52 units, built in 2015 by Ocorp Development.

Now a decade old, The Grove has settled into a solid mid-market position in North Glenmore — established enough to have a track record, recent enough not to need major updates.

Its Drysdale Boulevard location puts it close to Drysdale Row and the rest of North Glenmore's newer townhome cluster, giving buyers real comparables nearby when evaluating price.
