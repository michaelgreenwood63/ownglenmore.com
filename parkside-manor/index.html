Parkside Manor is a condo and townhome community at 1660 Ufton Court, in Glenmore's HUB area directly neighbouring the Parkinson Recreation Centre, a major civic facility underway in the neighbourhood.

Listings here have ranged from top-floor 2-bedroom condos around 1,324 sq ft up to larger 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom homes near 1,700 sq ft, giving the complex a wider size range than a typical single-format condo building — some units lean condo-style, others townhome-style.

The Parkinson Recreation Centre proximity is a genuine, growing draw — as that facility comes online, buildings directly next to it stand to see rising demand from buyers who want to be close to a major new amenity without paying downtown prices.
