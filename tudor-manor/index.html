Tudor Manor is a condo community at 1665 Ufton Court, a separate building from the neighbouring Parkside Manor at 1660 Ufton Court despite sharing the same court.

Listings here have included a 951 sq ft one-bedroom-plus-den unit with largely original finishes, giving a sense this is an older building where some units are still in as-built condition — the kind of place where renovation upside is part of the pitch.

The location puts residents close to Spall Plaza for everyday shopping, public transit, the Rail Trail for cycling and walking, and the new Parkinson Recreation Centre — a genuinely central spot within Glenmore.
