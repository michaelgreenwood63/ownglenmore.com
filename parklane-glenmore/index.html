Parklane is a townhome community at 115 Wyndham Crescent in North Glenmore — a lower-profile complex compared to some of the newer, larger builds nearby, but a real, established part of the neighbourhood's townhome stock.

We don't have a confirmed total unit count published for this complex, but active unit numbers we've seen run into the low 20s and higher — a mid-sized community, not a boutique handful of units.

As with any townhome complex where public unit-count data is thin, we can pull the strata's actual records before you make an offer so you're working from real numbers, not estimates.
