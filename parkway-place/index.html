Parkway Place is a 50-unit, boutique low-rise condo building at 1640 Ufton Court, part of Glenmore's older, established condo stock dating to the 1980s.

Units run primarily 2-bedroom layouts — a straightforward, no-frills format that tends to price below Glenmore's newer construction, appealing to buyers prioritizing value and location over modern finishes.

As with any 1980s-era strata, mechanical and building-envelope updates are worth asking about directly — we can help you get the strata's maintenance and depreciation report before you commit.
