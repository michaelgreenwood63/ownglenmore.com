Trellis is a 3-storey, concrete-construction townhome complex at 720 Valley Road, right at the corner of Valley Road and Glenmore Road — 49 units, built in 2022 by R&B Construction.

Concrete construction is genuinely notable for a townhome complex this size — most Glenmore townhomes from the same era are wood-frame. That generally means better sound separation between units and different long-term maintenance considerations.

Being directly across from Glenmore Central and close to The Verve, this stretch of Valley/Glenmore Road has become one of the more active pockets of newer construction in the neighbourhood.
