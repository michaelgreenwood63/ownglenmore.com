Glen Valley is a townhome community at 600 Boynton Place, next to Knox Mountain Park, known for architectural variety and geothermal heating and cooling across the development. The project won a 2012 Tommie Award, the Canadian Home Builders' Association's recognition for residential construction in the Okanagan.

Units here run larger than typical Glenmore condo stock — active listings have included 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom end units with side-by-side double garages and four-level townhomes topping 2,400 sq ft, with features like engineered flooring, stone-surround fireplaces, and private sundecks.

The Knox Mountain Park location is the draw for a lot of buyers here — direct proximity to trail access, plus the same central Glenmore convenience as the rest of the neighbourhood.

Glen Valley — FAQ

What makes Glen Valley different from other Glenmore townhome communities?
Glen Valley is known for its architectural variety and geothermal heating and cooling system, which is less common in Okanagan strata developments. It won a 2012 Tommie Award and sits directly next to Knox Mountain Park.
