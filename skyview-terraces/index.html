SkyView Terraces is a 3-storey townhome/condo complex at 1515 Highland Drive North, built in 2016 — 40 units total, on the Highland Drive edge of Glenmore.

One genuinely distinctive feature: geothermal heating, which most Glenmore buildings from the same era don't have — worth asking about the practical impact on utility costs if you're comparing it to a conventionally-heated building.

Highland Drive sits at the boundary of how different sources describe this pocket of Kelowna, so it's worth confirming exactly which amenities and schools apply to a specific unit here before you commit — we can walk you through that.
