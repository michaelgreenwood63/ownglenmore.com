The Verve is a large condo complex spanning 8 four-storey wood-frame buildings along Yates Road, at the corner of Glenmore Road — 441 units in total, making it one of the biggest condo communities in Glenmore. It was built by Mission Group, completed in 2008.

The complex has real amenity scale to match its size: a kidney-shaped outdoor pool, a beach volleyball court, and a dog park — the kind of shared space you don't typically get at a smaller Glenmore building.

With that many units across several buildings and addresses (individual buildings run from roughly 511 through 571 Yates Road), unit condition and layout vary more here than at a smaller complex — worth getting specifics on the exact building and unit before comparing prices.
