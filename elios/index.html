Elios is a townhouse complex at 218 Glen Park Drive, in the heart of Glenmore within walking distance of grocery stores, restaurants, coffee shops, trails, parks, and schools, with a quick commute to UBC Okanagan.

Listings here have featured open-concept main floors with gas fireplaces, large windows, wrap-around kitchens with dine-up bars and stainless appliances, and in at least one unit, a full unfinished basement offering roughly 625 sq ft of additional space to develop.

The location is the headline feature — Elios trades on being genuinely walkable to daily essentials in a neighbourhood where that's not a given, which matters most to buyers who want to cut down on car dependence.
