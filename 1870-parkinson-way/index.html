1870 Parkinson Way is a townhome community in central Glenmore. No distinct marketed building name has turned up in its listings yet, so we're using the civic address directly rather than guessing at one.

Listings here have described single-level, corner-unit townhomes around 1,200 sq ft, 2-bedroom and 2-bathroom layouts, with features like gas fireplaces, in-floor heating, and built-in closets showing up across different units.

It's a smaller, quieter complex compared to some of Glenmore's larger named buildings — if you're looking here, we can dig into the strata documents directly since there isn't much public information to go on beyond the listings themselves.
