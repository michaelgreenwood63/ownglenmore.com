Eminence at Knox Mountain is a 4-storey, 277-unit condo development at 630 Boynton Place — currently under construction and selling pre-completion, with the developer targeting 2026.

It's the largest new-construction project we're tracking in the area, and it sits right where Glenmore's northern edge meets Knox Mountain — close enough to Knox Mountain Park to draw on that as much as on Glenmore's own amenities.

Because it's still pre-completion, buying here means working through developer contracts and a construction timeline rather than a standard resale purchase — we can walk you through exactly how that process differs.
